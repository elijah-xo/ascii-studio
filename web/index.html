03 — TRANSLATE
What do these controls do?
Detail
More rows create finer dots. Try 32 for a bold avatar or 72 for a detailed portrait.
Highlights & total dot coverage
Highlights sets how much of the figure uses bright dots; total coverage includes shadow dots. Try 58% / 86%, then increase coverage if features disappear.
Softness
Smooths the source before making dots. Increase it to quiet texture; lower it to keep small details.
Local contrast
Brings out nearby light and dark detail. Increase it for flat lighting; reduce it if skin or edges look noisy.
Background removal
Remove dark tones suppresses low brightness, including dark subject details. For a light background, invert first. Transition softness fades the cutoff instead of making a hard edge.
BACKGROUND

Suppress dark tones. Invert first for a light background. This is tonal separation, not automatic cutout.

A bright subject on a dark ground works best. Your original stays untouched.
